      About the data

      Fishing information and coverage

      Fish Finder Ontario brings together publicly licensed provincial fisheries datasets and provides fishing information for planning and reference. Its regulation summaries are not legal documents or a complete record of current requirements.

      Municipal bylaws, park and conservation-area rules, private-property and access restrictions, posted signs and temporary closures may not be included. Recorded fish, stocking records, map pins and tackle recommendations do not establish permission to access a site or fish there. An open provincial season does not confirm that fishing is permitted at a particular location.

      Check the current official fishing regulations and notices, the rules of the relevant municipality or land manager, and posted signs before fishing. The app does not verify permission for every location. Data import dates and known limitations are listed below.

      Map locations

      Blue and green pins identify waterbodies. Gold icons identify boat launches; purple icons identify shoreline access, including docks and piers. Waterbody pins are reference locations, not confirmed access points. Ambiguous legacy locations are hidden pending review. Distances are straight-line distances.

      City searches use Natural Resources Canada's Geolocator service to place the grey search pin. This search requires an internet connection.

      Boat launches and shore access

      Access points come from Ontario's Fishing Access Point dataset. Only records marked for public map display are included. Ownership, parking, fees and accessibility are shown as recorded, including unknown values. Check current access and posted signs. A listed site is not a guarantee of free access or permission to fish.

      Site verification dates are shown separately when provided. Access points are not assigned fish species or linked to a waterbody solely by proximity.

      Species and stocking

      Species lists reflect available records. Stocking dates show the latest event included in the app, not a recent survey or a guarantee of fish currently present. Source dates are not available for every record.

      Fishing regulations

      Area descriptions were audited November 5, 2026. Complete wording from Ontario’s 2026 guide and official variation order restores 26 descriptions across 136 records. Three near-limit descriptions were verified as complete. One Zone 20 record remains explicitly flagged because its imported exclusions could not be reconciled confidently with the current guide. Area description audit and sources.

      Lake cards summarize the included 2026 provincial seasons, limits, waterbody exceptions and sanctuary notices. Select a fish and fishing date to see the relevant provincial season. Partial areas and uncertain matches require checking the exact official description. Rules are not automatically updated when the year changes.

      Waterbody coverage includes named lakes, rivers, creeks, bays and other waters from the Ontario Waterbody Location Identifier, plus the regulations location table. Fish and stocking records are joined by government waterbody ID. Missing fish records mean unknown species, not an empty waterbody. This is not a complete inventory of every Ontario waterbody. Bait and fisheries zones are based on official boundaries at each mapped point; check boundaries for large waters.

      Fish Finder Ontario is independently developed and is not endorsed by the Government of Ontario.

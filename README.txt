Crown land overlay update

Upload all files and the images folder from this ZIP to your existing GitHub Pages folder, overwriting matching files. Include the new lake-setups.js file. Keep index.html at the same path.

Choose Waterbodies, Boat launches or Shore access below the map. Each choice shows its own pins and cards. The main fish slider appears only when browsing waterbodies, and hides while a lake card is open.

Tap a pictured fish in a lake card for bait and tackle recommendations. Back to results restores your fish filter. Switching browsing choices keeps the map position and zoom.

Explore access points nearby starts with boat launches and keeps the chosen lake highlighted. Switch to Shore access to see shoreline locations. Back to waterbody restores the lake and previous map view.

Map key explains the symbols. Settings contains optional help and general fishing advice.

General bait and tackle recommendations are selected from the most frequently listed baits across the existing recommendation entries for that fish. Unspecified conditions are not filled in automatically.

Your existing saved spots and notes remain stored in the same browser on the same website address. Datasets are unchanged.

If the old layout appears after deployment, use Settings > Update Data & App, then reopen the app.

Maps, fonts, city lookup and interface dependencies still use online services, as in the previous app.

My location now shows a loading bar over the map while finding your position. It clears when the map updates or the request fails. Location requests time out after 20 seconds so the button can be tried again.

This update restores visible suggested colours, matches tackle image containers to their white backgrounds, fixes 20 image mappings, makes shore access clusters purple, and adds 12px spacing above Back to results on mobile. Where the source recommendations have no colour preference, the app says so.

Type three or more letters to see up to eight matching waterbodies. Select a suggestion to centre the map and open its lake card. Use arrow keys and Enter on desktop. Town searches still work with the search button or Enter without selecting a suggestion.

The Crown land button at the top right of the map toggles live Ontario Crown land records and dashed purple CLUPA Provincial policy boundaries. It starts off on each visit and works alongside Waterbodies, Boat launches and Shore access. Zoom in to level 7 or closer to load mapping. The information button explains the legend, source limitations and access cautions, and lets you hide the policy outlines. Tap the map while policy boundaries are enabled to look up a policy name/ID and open the official atlas for its report.

Green shading includes ministry unpatented land and acquisitions, including some lakebeds. It does not establish public access. Policy outlines are not ownership boundaries. The overlay needs an internet connection and shows a message with Retry if the Ontario mapping service cannot load. No Crown land geometry is bundled for offline use.
